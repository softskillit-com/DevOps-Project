Why Kubernetes?

Docker runs one container. Kubernetes runs — and keeps running — thousands of them, across many machines, without you watching every one.

🔄 Self-Healing

If a container crashes or a server dies, Kubernetes automatically creates a replacement — no alerts to answer at 3am, no manual restarts.

📈 Real Auto-Scaling

Unlike plain Docker, Kubernetes can watch CPU/memory usage and automatically add or remove replicas as traffic rises and falls.

🚀 Zero-Downtime Deploys

Rolling updates replace old Pods with new ones gradually, so users never see a broken or offline app during a release.

🌐 Multi-Machine by Design

A cluster can span dozens or thousands of servers, and Kubernetes decides where each container runs — you just declare what you want.

🔐 Built-In Secrets & Config

ConfigMaps and Secrets separate configuration and credentials from your code, without baking them into images.

💼 Industry Standard

Used by Google, Netflix, Spotify and most modern tech companies — it's the most in-demand orchestration skill in DevOps job postings.
